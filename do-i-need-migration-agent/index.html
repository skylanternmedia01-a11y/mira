Do I Need a Migration Agent for My Visa Application?

Not always. Some visa applications are straightforward enough to handle yourself. Others are complex enough that professional help is worth every cent. Here is an honest breakdown of when you need an agent and when you can save the money.

When You Probably Do Not Need an Agent

Simple visitor visa applications from low-risk countries, straightforward student visa applications with a clear GTE case, working holiday visa applications, and simple citizenship applications where you clearly meet the residency requirement. If your situation is uncomplicated, the Department's online application system is user-friendly and the instructions are clear.

When an Agent Is Worth It

Partner visas (the evidence requirements are extensive and the stakes are high), skilled visas (points strategy, skills assessments, and EOI timing), employer sponsored visas (coordination between you and your employer), any application after a previous refusal, applications with health or character concerns, business and investment visas (the financial stakes are too high for mistakes), and protection visa claims (the legal complexity requires experienced representation).

The Real Cost of Getting It Wrong

A refused visa application costs you the application fee (which can be thousands of dollars), months of processing time, and potentially a refusal on your immigration record that complicates future applications. For complex visas, the cost of an agent is typically a fraction of the cost of a refusal. Think of it as insurance — you may not need it, but if your application has any complexity, the peace of mind and expertise is worth the investment.

A Middle Ground — Agent Review

Some agents offer a review-only service where you prepare the application yourself and they review it before lodgement. This costs less than full representation and catches common errors. It is a good option for moderately complex applications where you are confident in the substance but want professional quality assurance.
