Understanding Parent Visa Options

Australia offers several parent visa pathways. The contributory parent visa (subclass 143 onshore, 173 temporary) costs approximately $49,000–$55,000 in government charges alone but processes in around 5 to 7 years. The non-contributory parent visa (subclass 103) costs approximately $6,000 but has a wait time that currently exceeds 29 years. The aged parent visa (subclass 804) is for parents already in Australia who are old enough for the Australian aged pension. Your agent will assess which pathway makes sense given your parents' age, financial situation, and urgency.

Balance of Family Test and Sponsorship

All parent visas require the balance of family test — at least half of the sponsor's children must be Australian permanent residents or citizens, or more children must live in Australia than any other single country. The sponsoring child must also meet an assurance of support requirement, essentially a bond held by the government. An agent ensures you meet these requirements and helps you understand the financial commitment before lodging.

Why Specialist Agents Matter for Parent Visas

The stakes are high with parent visas. The combined government and agent fees can exceed $55,000, and a refusal means losing the application charge. Parent visa agents help you assess eligibility before committing to the costs, prepare health and character evidence (critical for older applicants), manage the multi-year processing period, and handle any requests for additional information that arise during what is often a very long wait.
