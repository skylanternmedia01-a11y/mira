When You Need an Agent for a Student Visa

Many student visa applications are simple enough to self-lodge. But certain situations almost always benefit from professional help: if you have a previous visa refusal or cancellation, if you are changing education levels (for example, moving from a Master's to a diploma, which raises GTE concerns), if you have gaps in your study or work history, if you are from a higher-risk country, or if your financial evidence is complex. An agent experienced in student visas knows exactly how to frame the GTE statement for your specific situation.

The Genuine Temporary Entrant Requirement

The GTE requirement is where most student visa applications succeed or fail. The Department assesses whether you genuinely intend to stay temporarily in Australia for study purposes. They consider your circumstances in your home country, your immigration history, the course you have chosen relative to your qualifications, and your potential situation in Australia. Applicants from certain countries or with certain profiles face higher scrutiny. A specialist agent drafts your GTE statement to address the Department's specific concerns for your profile.

Financial Capacity and Health Insurance

You must demonstrate access to sufficient funds for tuition, living costs, and return travel. The current living cost threshold is approximately $24,505 per year. Evidence can include bank statements, loan approvals, scholarship letters, or government sponsor documentation. You also need Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the duration of your visa. An agent ensures your financial evidence is presented correctly and covers any gaps.
