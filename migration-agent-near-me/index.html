Local vs Remote — Does It Matter?

Almost all migration agent work is done electronically — document review, application lodgement, Department correspondence. You do not need to visit your agent's office for most visa types. What matters is their specialisation in your visa type and the quality of their communication, not their proximity. That said, some clients prefer a face-to-face initial consultation, which is perfectly reasonable.

How to Find Local Agents

Search the MARA register filtered by your state or city. Look for agents who list your visa type as a specialisation. Check online reviews on Google and community forums. Ask within your community — particularly if you have a specific cultural or language preference. Or use a matching service like ours to be connected with agents who handle your visa type regardless of location.

Why Specialisation Beats Proximity

An agent three suburbs away who primarily handles student visas is not the best choice for your partner visa application — even if they are convenient. An agent in another state who handles 50 partner visas a year will give you better advice and a stronger application. Prioritise expertise over location.
