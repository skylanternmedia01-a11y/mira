What Affordable Actually Looks Like

The cheapest agents charge around $800 for student visas and $2,000 for partner or skilled visas. These are not necessarily lower quality — some agents with lower overheads (working from home, smaller teams) can offer competitive rates while maintaining expertise. The key is to check their experience with your visa type, not just their price.

Free and Low-Cost Options

Community legal centres in most states offer free immigration assistance for eligible clients. Legal aid services cover protection visa claims. Some migration agents offer free initial consultations. Migration resource centres funded by the Department provide settlement assistance. If cost is a significant barrier, explore these options before assuming you cannot access professional help.

When Cheap Becomes Expensive

An agent who charges $1,500 but lodges an incomplete application that results in a request for additional information and a 6-month delay has cost you far more than an agent who charges $3,000 and gets it right the first time. A refused application costs you the entire government application charge — often thousands of dollars — plus the time and emotional cost of starting over.

How to Reduce Costs

Ask about review-only services (you prepare, they review). Ask about payment plans. Get quotes from multiple agents and negotiate. Handle document gathering yourself rather than paying the agent's time for administrative tasks. Be organised — agents who spend less time chasing you for documents can charge less.
