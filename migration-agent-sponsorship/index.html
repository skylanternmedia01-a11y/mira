Employer Sponsorship

For employer-sponsored visas (482, 186, 494), your agent works with both you and your employer. They guide your employer through the sponsorship application, lodge the nomination, and prepare your visa application. Many employers have never sponsored before and need hand-holding through the process. An agent who manages both sides prevents the common situation where the employer's paperwork holds up your visa.

Family Sponsorship

Partner visas, parent visas, and some family visas require an Australian sponsor — your partner, child, or other family member. The sponsor must meet income, character, and undertaking requirements. Your agent prepares both the sponsor's and applicant's documentation, ensuring alignment.

State and Territory Nomination

Skilled visas (190, 491) require state or territory nomination. While this is not traditional sponsorship, the process of securing nomination adds a layer of complexity. Your agent identifies which state is most likely to nominate you and prepares your nomination application alongside your visa application.
