What Affects Your Processing Time

The main factors are: completeness of your application (incomplete applications face delays from RFIs), security checking requirements (varies by nationality and circumstances), health assessment follow-ups, character issues requiring further assessment, and the Department's current workload and staffing for your visa category.

What Your Agent Can Do

Your agent cannot speed up the Department's queue. They can submit a complete application that avoids RFI delays, monitor your application status, lodge processing enquiries at appropriate intervals, and advise on any legitimate priority processing options. The most impactful thing an agent does for processing time is getting the application right the first time.

Setting Realistic Expectations

Be cautious of anyone — agent or otherwise — who promises specific processing dates. Processing times are ranges, not guarantees. Your agent should give you a realistic estimate based on current trends for your visa type and flag any factors that might extend processing.
