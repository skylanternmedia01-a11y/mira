Fee Ranges by Visa Category

Student visas: $800–$2,500. Visitor visas: $500–$1,500. Partner visas: $2,500–$6,000. Skilled visas (189/190/491): $2,000–$5,000. Employer sponsored (482/186): $2,500–$6,000. Parent visas: $3,000–$7,000. Business/investment visas: $5,000–$15,000+. Citizenship: $500–$2,000. AAT review/appeals: $3,000–$10,000+. These are agent professional fees only — government application charges are additional.

What Drives Fees Up or Down

Simpler cases within each category cost less. Factors that increase fees include: prior visa refusals, complex relationship evidence (de facto, same-sex partnerships with limited documentation), multiple family members, health or character concerns, AAT review applications, and cases requiring ministerial intervention. The agent's fee reflects the amount of work your specific case requires.

Government Charges Are Separate

Do not confuse agent fees with government application charges. The government charges for a partner visa are approximately $8,850. Skilled visa charges range from $4,000 to $4,500. Business visas range from $6,000 to $9,000+. Parent visa contributory charges exceed $49,000. Your agent's fee is on top of these government charges.

How to Compare Fees Fairly

When comparing agents, ensure you are comparing like for like. Ask each agent for a written scope of work alongside their fee. One agent quoting $3,000 including skills assessment help may be better value than another quoting $2,000 without it. The total cost — including any extras — is what matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Agents set their own fees. However, OMARA requires that fees are fair and clearly communicated. If you believe an agent has charged unfairly, you can lodge a complaint with OMARA.

Most agents require a deposit (30–50%) with the balance due at lodgement. Be cautious of agents who require 100% payment before any work begins. Payment terms should be in your written fee agreement.

Some community legal centres offer free or low-cost immigration assistance. Legal aid may be available for protection visa claims. Additionally, comparing quotes from multiple agents may reveal more affordable options for your specific case.

Ask about fees for: skills assessment assistance, additional family members, responding to requests for additional information, AAT review if refused, translation and certification of documents, and any administrative or file management fees. A good agent discloses all fees upfront.
