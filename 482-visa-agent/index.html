482 Visa Streams and Eligibility

The 482 visa has three streams: the Short-term stream (up to 2 years, limited renewal), the Medium-term stream (up to 4 years, pathway to permanent residency via 186), and the Labour Agreement stream (for employers with a labour agreement). Your occupation must appear on the relevant occupation list, and your employer must be an approved sponsor. The Skills in Demand visa is progressively replacing the 482 with salary-based streams — your agent will advise which pathway applies to you.

Employer Sponsorship Requirements

Your employer must be approved as a standard business sponsor, lodge a nomination for your specific role at the market salary rate, and demonstrate labour market testing (advertising the role to Australians first). Many employers have never sponsored before — an experienced agent walks them through the process and ensures compliance from day one.

Pathway to Permanent Residency

The Medium-term stream of the 482 provides a pathway to the 186 visa (permanent residency) after working for your employer for 2–3 years. This transition stream is one of the most popular routes to PR in Australia. Your agent should be planning the 186 transition from the moment your 482 is lodged.
