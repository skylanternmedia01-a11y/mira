Business and Investment Visa Pathways

The main pathways are the Business Innovation stream (subclass 188A — minimum $250,000 investment in a new or existing business), the Investor stream (188B — $2.5 million in complying investments), the Significant Investor stream (188C — $5 million in complying investments), and the Premium Investor stream (188D — $15 million). Each leads to permanent residency through the 888 visa after meeting activity and residency requirements. State nomination is required for most streams, and each state has different priority sectors and requirements.

Why Business Visas Demand Specialist Agents

Business visa applications involve detailed business plans, financial audits, investment structuring, state nomination applications, and ongoing compliance monitoring. The agents who handle these cases typically have backgrounds in business migration and work alongside accountants, business advisors, and investment managers. The wrong investment structure or an incomplete business plan can result in a refusal that costs far more than the agent's fee.

State Nomination and Investment Compliance

Each Australian state and territory has different nomination priorities for business visas. Some prioritise manufacturing, others technology or agriculture. Your agent needs to match your business background and investment plans with the state most likely to nominate you. After grant, you must meet ongoing requirements — minimum turnover, employment of Australian staff, and residency obligations — to qualify for the 888 permanent visa.
