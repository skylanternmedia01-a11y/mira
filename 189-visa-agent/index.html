How the 189 Works

The 189 is a points-tested visa that grants permanent residency without requiring sponsorship from an employer or state government. You submit an Expression of Interest through SkillSelect with your points claim. When your points score is high enough for your occupation, you receive an invitation to apply. The minimum passmark is 65 points, but most occupations require 75–90+ points to receive an invitation. An agent assesses your true points position and advises whether the 189 is realistic or whether the 190 or 491 offers a faster pathway.

Points Optimisation Strategy

An experienced 189 agent does not just count your current points — they identify opportunities to increase them. This might include improving your English test score (PTE, IELTS, or TOEFL), obtaining a professional year qualification, gaining Australian work experience, applying for a higher qualification recognition, or claiming partner points. Even 5 additional points can be the difference between waiting years for an invitation and receiving one in months.

Skills Assessment and Occupation Lists

Your occupation must appear on the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL). You need a positive skills assessment from the relevant authority — ACS for IT, Engineers Australia for engineering, VETASSESS for many other occupations, TRA for trades. Each authority has different requirements. An agent who works with your occupation regularly knows exactly what evidence to provide and how to maximise your assessed experience.
